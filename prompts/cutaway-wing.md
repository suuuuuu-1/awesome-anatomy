## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Build an interactive aircraft wing cutaway. Show the spars, ribs, skin, flaps, and slats, with controls to compare rectangular, tapered, and swept wings and extend the high-lift devices. Explain what changes, state the limits of the drawing, include sources, and deliver a self-contained HTML file.
```
