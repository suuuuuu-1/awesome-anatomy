## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Build an interactive cutaway of a 4-cylinder boxer engine with horizontally opposed pistons, connecting rods, and separate crankpins. Let me turn the crankshaft and follow an opposing piston pair to see why the pistons move together. Explain the simplifications, include sources, and deliver self-contained HTML.
```
