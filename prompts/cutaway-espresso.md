## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Build a cutaway espresso machine with a reservoir, pump, boiler, brew valve, group head, and portafilter. Trace the water into the cup, with controls to step through heating and brewing and select parts to read about them. Explain the simplified plumbing, include sources, and deliver a self-contained HTML file.
```
