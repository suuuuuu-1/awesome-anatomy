## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Draw a small single-purpose industrial machine, isometric, that takes one four-part fact card and files it. A hopper of stacked cards on the left feeds a toothed guard wheel engraved with a short code; a duplicate card is turned aside into a small reject tray. A two-column press in the middle stamps a card flat into a long ribbon of words, and a diverter sends the ribbon to two index bins, one a drawer of cards and one a tape spool. At the front a six-station turntable, one seat per ordering of the three parts, receives the card. Muted four-tone metal, one tiny engraved label, no diagram arrows or captions; the machine should look like it exists. Output one self-contained interactive HTML page: stepping across the bench advances a card through the stages and lights the next turntable seat.
```
