## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Draw a small single-purpose industrial machine, isometric, that physically fuses two ranked lists. Two tall open magazines of flat slips—one labelled for keyword search, one for vector search—feed a slotted revolver drum in the middle that interleaves the two rankings into one queue on a rail, best slip first. A carriage with a down-pointing needle slides along the rail, a round dial reads a fused score, and from the top slip a thread runs through two eyelets to an answer card clipped on a board at the back. Muted four-tone metal, one tiny engraved label, no diagram arrows or captions; the machine should look like it exists. Output one self-contained interactive HTML page: hovering across the rail parks the carriage and updates the needle and readout.
```
