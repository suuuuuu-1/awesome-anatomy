## Published prompt / 公开提示词

Author-published text; completeness and reproduction have not been independently established. / 作者公开文本，未独立确认是否包含全部修改过程或可直接复现。

```text
Build an interactive GE9X cutaway using GE Aerospace’s published references. Show its 16-blade fan, 3-stage booster, 11-stage high-pressure compressor, TAPS III combustor, 2-stage high-pressure turbine, 6-stage low-pressure turbine, and 2 shafts, with separate core and bypass airflow. Let me select parts and pause the flow. Explain the simplifications, cite sources, and deliver self-contained HTML.
```
