# Contributing

Suggest an Anatomy work, SVG asset or showcase by editing the relevant README section or opening an issue.

Include:

- A short title and one sentence explaining what the work shows.
- The original creator and source post or page.
- A live example, preview, source code or published prompt when available.
- Known format, reuse terms and any missing resource links.

A demonstration-only work is welcome when clearly labeled. Do not invent prompts, duplicate reposts, count color themes as separate works, or claim a work was tested when it was not. Keep third-party media at its existing source unless its author permits redistribution.

For corrections or removal requests, identify the entry and the original creator.

Update the matching record in `data/entries.json` and keep both READMEs consistent. Keep the entry and category counts correct. Published prompts should match their original source.
